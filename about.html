The honest pivot

Google did the reading. Now AI search does. And it actually cares.

Let's be honest. Nobody read the affiliate copy. They skimmed the headline, clocked the bonus, and clicked. Google parsed the keywords and ranked the page. Everyone got what they needed. Content was just the middle step nobody thought about too hard.

Then AI search showed up and started actually reading. And it turns out it's pretty good at spotting the difference between something written for a crawl budget and something written for a person.

Now the thing nobody was doing (building trust, earning credibility, creating content that real people actually engage with) is the whole game. Communities. Authority. Content that means something to someone.

We've always written for humans. That's the whole model. In an AIO world, that's not just good practice; it's the strategy. Turns out the algorithm finally caught up.
