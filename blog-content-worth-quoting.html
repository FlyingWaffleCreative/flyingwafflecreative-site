Content Strategy SEO AIO June 2026

Stop Publishing. Start Being Worth Quoting.

Content isn't competing for clicks anymore — it's competing to be cited. Here's why the standard has changed, and what that means for every piece of content your brand puts into the world.

By Lara Wilson Flying Waffle Creative

Content Is King. But Kings Can Be Dethroned.

"Content is king" used to be a throwaway line agencies put on a slide and moved past. Today, it's closer to a survival requirement — and the rules of the game have changed completely.

The Shift Nobody Briefed You On

Here's the shift: your content isn't competing for clicks anymore. It's competing to be cited.

AI-generated answers and search snippets are increasingly the first thing people see, and they pull from sources that demonstrate real expertise and credibility. Search has moved from "what ranks" to "what gets referenced" — and if your content isn't trustworthy enough to be quoted, it's not going to get you results.

Google's AI Overviews, Perplexity, ChatGPT — they don't reward volume. They reward authority. They pull from content that is specific, well-sourced, and clearly written by someone who actually knows what they're talking about. Thin content, keyword-stuffed pages, and generic takes don't make the cut. They get skipped.

Purpose Before Publishing

That's the standard we hold ourselves to at Flying Waffle Creative. We don't write articles or build infographics just to fill a content calendar or "naturally" stuff keywords. Every piece has a job to do.

Content without a clear purpose won't present itself as useful or credible — so it doesn't get cited, shared, or ranked. It just takes up space. And in a landscape where AI is increasingly deciding what's worth surfacing to your audience, taking up space isn't a strategy.

Strategy First. Always.

This is where strategy comes before a single word gets written. We start by understanding what you're actually trying to achieve, then build content designed to be found, trusted, and referenced — by search engines, by AI systems summarizing the web, and by the players you're trying to reach.

No two clients get the same playbook. What earns trust and traffic for one brand falls flat for another — different audiences, different industries, different ways of getting discovered online. So our approach is built around your goals, not a generic template.

The Bar

The result of that approach: content that doesn't just rank — it becomes a source worth citing. That's the bar we hold ourselves to, and we don't settle for less.

Because in 2026, being publishable isn't enough. You have to be quotable.
