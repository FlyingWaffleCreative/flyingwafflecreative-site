Content Strategy SEO AIO June 2026

Stop Publishing. Start Being Worth Quoting.

Content isn't competing for clicks anymore it's competing to be cited. Here's why the standard has changed, and what that means for every piece of content your brand puts into the world.

Flying Waffle Creative

Here's the shift: your content isn't competing for clicks anymore. It's competing to be cited.

AI-generated answers and search snippets are increasingly the first thing people see, and they pull from sources that demonstrate real expertise and credibility. Search has moved from "what ranks" to "what gets referenced" and if your content isn't trustworthy enough to be quoted, it's not going to get you results.

That's the standard we hold ourselves to. We don't write articles or build infographics just to fill a content calendar or 'naturally' stuff keywords. Every piece has a job to do. Content without a clear purpose won't present itself as useful or credible, so it doesn't get cited, shared, or ranked.

This is where strategy comes before a single word gets written. We start by understanding what you're actually trying to achieve, then build content designed to be found, trusted, and referenced by search engines, by AI systems summarizing the web, and by the players you're trying to reach.
